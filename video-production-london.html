Filming in London: what to know.

  • Every borough runs its own filming.London has 33 local authorities, each with its own film office or service. Film London doesn’t issue permits itself, but points you to the right one. We work out which office applies before the shoot and make the application.
  • Small crews have it easier.Film London’s guidance for a crew of five or fewer, handheld and not blocking the pavement, is that formal permission usually isn’t needed on the street, but the borough still wants at least three days’ notice. Some councils charge an admin fee anyway.
  • The City and the Royal Parks are separate.The City of London charges even small handheld crews and wants a week’s notice for any tripod. Hyde Park, Regent’s Park, Richmond Park and the other Royal Parks need their own licence.
  • Red routes need TfL.Filming beside a red route means telling Transport for London as well as the borough, again with at least three days’ notice. We plan around main roads where we can.
  • Indoors keeps it simple.Inside your shop, studio, kitchen or office, the council isn’t involved. Most interviews happen indoors, and we only take the camera onto the street when the story needs it.

Do I need a permit to film in London?

It depends on the borough and where the camera goes. On the street, most boroughs want notice even for a small handheld crew, and some charge an admin fee. The City of London and the Royal Parks have their own rules. Inside your own premises, no council permit is needed. We check which office applies and make the application.

Where are you based?

South London, close to Croydon and Crystal Palace. That makes South London and Surrey quick to reach, and we film across the whole city, from Enfield to Kingston.

Have you filmed across London?

Yes. Our published London work includes La Fromagerie, a cheese business that began in Highbury, the shoemakers Carreducker at the Oxo Tower, char.gy’s B Corp film by Monument, a residential project in Kensington for Easee, a drainage job in Enfield and renovations in Tooting and East Dulwich.

How much does a brand film cost in London?

Our documentary brand films start from £15,000 + VAT, and most land between £15,000 and £25,000 + VAT. Every film is quoted as one fixed figure after a short call, because a one-location film and a multi-location film aren’t the same job. Research, shooting, edit, grade, mix and the full licence are all inside it. If a film isn’t the right spend yet, our one-off Spotlight shoot is £795 + VAT, a Content Day is £1,200 + VAT and case study films start from £2,500 + VAT.

How long does a brand documentary take?

It depends on the scope. A film can take days, weeks or months, from a single location shot in a few days to a story followed over a season. We set the timeline with you on the first call, and most of the quality lives in the part that can’t be rushed: the research before filming and the edit after it.

Can you film social media content for a London business?

Yes. A Content Day is £1,200 + VAT and gives you a batch of short videos and photographs from one visit, edited and ready to post. If you’d rather not run the accounts yourself, our monthly plans cover the planning and posting too.

Who is in it, what they do, and why you think there is a story there. We come back within one working day, and if we do not think there is a film in it we will say so.
