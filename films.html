A documentary, not an advert.

An advert tells people what a company does. A film makes them understand why anyone bothers. That difference is mostly made before the camera comes out, in the time spent working out which story is actually the one.

It is usually not the product. It is the person who started it, the thing that nearly went wrong, the part of the process almost nobody outside gets to see, or the customer whose life quietly changed. Those films get watched to the end, sent on to other people, and still work in three years.

We film them the way a documentary is filmed. Real conversations, real environments, nothing read off an autocue, and enough room in the edit for the film to breathe.

The ambition is that someone watches the film and feels something, understands something, or sees the company differently afterwards.

Where your film gets shown.

Most production companies hand over a file and leave. We plan where the film goes from the first call, so it reaches people who’ve never heard of you, not just the ones who already follow you.

  • An exclusive premiereWe pitch the finished film to one publication that fits your story, from a national title to the leading specialist one in your world, for an exclusive first showing.
  • A press storyA story with the film at its centre, built on your real hook: an anniversary, a milestone, a first look inside. Taken to the journalists who cover your sector.
  • Film festivalsShort documentary programmes in the UK and abroad. We plan the festival run with you and handle the entries.
  • Film awardsBranded film and industry awards where the film has a real chance, so the work keeps earning attention after launch.

You can use the film too.

The documentary comes first, made to premiere and travel. But it’s yours to use as well, on your website and in sales, recruitment and events, and it can be cut down for social. We agree exactly what you need on the call, so you only pay for what you’ll use.

  • Your website
  • Sales conversations
  • Recruitment
  • Events and screenings
  • Social cut-downs
  • Stills

We won’t promise a placement. No one can honestly guarantee that a publication runs a film or a festival selects it. What we do promise is a film made to be worth showing, and a proper plan to show it.

How we make your film.

Four stages. The first is the one most people skip, and it's the one that decides whether the film is any good.

  • Research and accessTime with you before anything's filmed. Who's in it, what they've been through, what nobody outside the company knows. We come back with the story we think it is, and you tell us whether we're right.
  • FilmingAs long as the story needs, from a few days in one place to weeks across several. A cinema camera and proper sound. Interviews that are conversations, the work happening as it actually happens, and stills captured across the same days.
  • Editing the storyCut as a story, not a list of features. You see it at rough cut, while changing it is still cheap, and again before it's finished.
  • Colour and soundColour graded and sound mixed by people who do only that. It's the difference between a film that looks expensive and one that looks like a video.

  • Someone in the business genuinely cares.A founder with a story, a craft that takes years to learn, a customer whose life changed, or a reason the company exists beyond the margin.

How much does a film cost?

Every film is quoted as one fixed figure after a short call, because a one-location film and a three-country film aren't the same job. Research, shooting, edit, grade, mix and the full licence are all inside it.

How long does a film take to make?

It depends on the scope. A film can take days, weeks or months, from a single location shot in a few days to a story followed over a season. We set the timeline with you on the first call, and filming abroad adds time for permits.

Do you film outside the UK?

Yes. We're based in London and film across the UK, Europe, the US and the Gulf. Travel, permits and local crew are built into the same fixed figure, quoted in pounds, dollars or dirhams.

Is the film scripted?

No. Interviews are conversations, the work is filmed as it actually happens, and nothing is read off an autocue. The structure comes from the research, not a script.

Do we get to approve the film?

Yes. You see the film at rough cut, while changes are still cheap, and again before it's final. Nothing goes out that you haven't signed off.

Who owns the film?

You get a full licence, indefinitely, for every platform including paid ads. With your permission we'll also show it as our own work.

What’s the difference between a brand documentary and a corporate documentary?

Not much in how it’s made. Both are real people, real work and no script. A corporate documentary is usually made for staff, investors or a tender, while a brand documentary is made to be watched by the public. We make both, and the research, shoot and edit are the same.

Where does the film premiere?

We pick one publication that fits your story, a national title or the leading specialist one in your sector, and pitch it the film as an exclusive. The press story and festival run follow from there.

Can you guarantee coverage or a festival selection?

No, and nobody honestly can. Editors and festival programmers decide. What we control is a film worth showing, a real news angle, and a proper plan to put it in front of the right people.

Tell us about your business

Who is in it, what they do, and why you think there is a story there. We come back within one working day, and if we do not think there is a film in it we will say so.
