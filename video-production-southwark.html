Filming in Southwark: what to know.

  • Southwark has its own film office.Street filming goes through the council’s filming service. We make the application as part of the job.
  • Small crews still tell the council.Film London’s guidance is that a handheld crew of five or fewer, not blocking the pavement, can usually film on the street, but Southwark still asks for notice, at least three days, and charges an admin fee.
  • Busy spots need an early start.Borough Market and the Thames Path are crowded most of the day, and private sites have their own say on filming, so we plan those shots for early mornings and ask first.
  • Indoors keeps it simple.Inside your shop, studio, kitchen or office, the council isn’t involved. Most interviews happen indoors, and we only take the camera onto the street when the story needs it.

Do I need a permit to film in Southwark?

If we’re filming on the street, Southwark wants notice even for a small handheld crew, at least three days, and there’s an admin fee. If we’re filming inside your premises, no council permit is needed. We handle the application when the shoot needs one.

Have you filmed in Southwark before?

Yes. Our Barry Road renovation film was shot in East Dulwich, and we filmed the bespoke shoemakers Carreducker in their workshop at the Oxo Tower on the South Bank.

Are you close to Southwark?

Yes. We’re based in South London, so Peckham, Dulwich and Bermondsey are a short drive, and we can be set up early without the travel eating into the day.

How much does a brand film cost in Southwark?

Our documentary brand films start from £15,000 + VAT, and most land between £15,000 and £25,000 + VAT. Every film is quoted as one fixed figure after a short call, because a one-location film and a multi-location film aren’t the same job. Research, shooting, edit, grade, mix and the full licence are all inside it. If a film isn’t the right spend yet, our one-off Spotlight shoot is £795 + VAT, a Content Day is £1,200 + VAT and case study films start from £2,500 + VAT.

How long does a brand documentary take?

It depends on the scope. A film can take days, weeks or months, from a single location shot in a few days to a story followed over a season. We set the timeline with you on the first call, and most of the quality lives in the part that can’t be rushed: the research before filming and the edit after it.

Can you film social media content for a Southwark business?

Yes. A Content Day is £1,200 + VAT and gives you a batch of short videos and photographs from one visit, edited and ready to post. If you’d rather not run the accounts yourself, our monthly plans cover the planning and posting too.

Tell us about your business

Who is in it, what they do, and why you think there is a story there. We come back within one working day, and if we do not think there is a film in it we will say so.
