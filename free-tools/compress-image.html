Compress images.

Shrink JPG, PNG and WebP files for your website, emails and socials. Do a whole batch at once and see how much you saved.

Your images will be listed here.
    Quality

    75 is a sweet spot for photos. Lower means smaller files and softer detail.

    WebP is usually the smallest and works in every modern browser. Shrinking huge photos to 1920 px wide saves the most.

    How it works

    1. Add your images

      Drop in one or a hundred. They stay on your device.

    2. Set the quality

      Pick a quality level, and optionally a new format or a maximum width.

    3. Download

      Check the savings per file, then save them one by one or as a ZIP.

    Good to know. PNGs are compressed by reducing them to a smaller colour palette, like TinyPNG does, which suits graphics and screenshots better than photos. If a file is already smaller than we can make it, we give you the original back rather than a bigger copy. Photo metadata such as location and camera details is removed.

    Questions

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Compression happens in your browser, so private photos never leave your device.

    What quality should I choose?

    For photos on a website, 70 to 80 looks great and saves a lot. For images people will zoom into, go 85 or higher.

    Why didn’t my PNG get much smaller?

    Photos saved as PNG are hard to shrink without changing format. Save them as JPG or WebP for a much bigger saving.

    Does it remove location data?

    Yes. Re-saving the image drops the EXIF data, including GPS location.
