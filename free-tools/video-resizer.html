Download↓

Output format. MP4 in Chrome, Edge and Safari; WebM in Firefox. Export runs in real time, so a 30 second clip takes about 30 seconds. Keep this tab open and in view until it’s finished.

Fill or fit?

Fill crops into the frame, which suits people and products in the middle of the shot. Fit keeps the whole picture and fills the gaps with a soft blur of the same clip.

Which size where

9:16 for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Stories. 4:5 takes up the most room in the Instagram and Facebook feed. 1:1 is the safe all-rounder. 16:9 for YouTube and websites.

Quality

Exports at about 8 Mbps, which is plenty for social. If your browser can’t open a file (some iPhone HEVC clips on Windows), convert it to a standard MP4 first.
