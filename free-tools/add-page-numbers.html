Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser using open-source libraries, so your file never leaves your device. You can even disconnect from the internet once the page has loaded and it’ll still work.

How do I skip the cover page?

Set “Start numbering on page” to 2. If you want that page to show 1, leave “First number to show” as 1.

Will the numbers cover my content?

They sit in the margin, so on most documents they’re clear of the text. If your pages run right to the edge, try Roomy or a different corner.

Does it work on landscape or rotated pages?

Yes. The numbers follow each page as it’s shown, so they land in the right place on landscape and rotated pages too.
