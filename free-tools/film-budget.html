Why two quotes can be miles apart

Two companies can quote £3,000 and £30,000 for what sounds like the same brief. The gap is nearly always research, shoot days and edit time, the work you don’t see on screen.

The quickest check

If a quote allows two days of editing for a six minute documentary, it’s a quote for a different kind of film.

Don’t forget VAT

Most UK production companies quote excluding VAT. If your organisation can’t reclaim it, which is common for some charities, budget for the gross figure.
