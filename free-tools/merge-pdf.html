Merge PDFs into one file.

Put proposals, invoices and appendices together in the right order, then save one tidy PDF. It all happens on your computer.

    Download↓

    How it works

    1. Add your PDFs

      Drop in two or more files. Each one shows its page count and first page.

    2. Set the order

      Drag them, or use the arrows, until they’re in the order you want.

    3. Merge and download

      Press Merge PDFs and save the combined file.

    Good to know. Bookmarks and fillable form fields from the original files may not carry over into the merged PDF. Page content, links and images do.

    Questions

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs entirely in your browser using open-source libraries, so your file never leaves your device. You can even disconnect from the internet once the page has loaded and it’ll still work.

    Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

    No set limit. It’s down to your device’s memory, so dozens of normal documents are fine. A pile of huge scanned files may be slow on a phone.

    Can I merge only some pages from a file?

    Merge the whole files here, then use Delete pages or Extract pages on the result to keep just the pages you want.

    Can it open password-protected PDFs?

    Not yet. Remove the password first (open it in your PDF reader and print or save a copy without protection), then drop the unlocked copy in here.
