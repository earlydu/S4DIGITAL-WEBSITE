How it works

  1. Add your GIF

    Drop in an animated GIF. It plays in the preview straight away.

  2. Write your caption

    Type top and bottom text and adjust the size, colour and outline as you watch.

  3. Download

    The text is drawn onto every frame and the GIF is rebuilt.

Good to know. The text sits in the same place on every frame. Fonts come from your device, so Impact looks slightly different on phones that don’t have it.

Questions

Can I put text in the middle?

Use the distance from edge slider to move both captions in towards the centre.

Will the GIF lose quality?

It’s rebuilt with up to 256 colours per frame, the same as any GIF, so it should look the same apart from your text.

Is my GIF uploaded?

No. The captions are drawn in your browser.
