      Private by design

      Files are read and rebuilt on your device with open-source libraries (pdf-lib and PDF.js). Nothing is sent anywhere.

      Locked files

      Password-protected or encrypted PDFs need unlocking first. Fillable form fields may not stay fillable after merging or extracting.

      Big files

      It runs on your machine, so very large PDFs (hundreds of pages, heavy scans) can take a moment. Your phone can do it too, just slower.
