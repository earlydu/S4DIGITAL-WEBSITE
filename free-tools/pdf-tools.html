Free PDF tools that don’t upload your files.

Merge, split, compress, sign and convert PDFs without sending them anywhere. Every tool runs in your browser, so contracts, invoices and client decks stay on your computer.

Organise

Merge PDFJoin several PDFs into one, in the order you choose. Split PDFBreak a PDF into ranges, single pages or equal chunks. Extract pagesPick the pages you need and save them as a new PDF. Delete pagesRemove blank, duplicate or unwanted pages from a PDF. Organise PDFDrag pages into a new order, rotate or remove them. Rotate PDFTurn sideways or upside-down pages the right way up.

Compress and edit

Compress PDFShrink a heavy PDF so it fits in an email. Add page numbersNumber your pages in the style and spot you want. Watermark PDFStamp DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or your name across pages. Sign PDFDraw or type your signature and place it on the page. Crop PDFTrim margins or cut a PDF down to the part you need.

Convert to PDF

JPG to PDFTurn photos and screenshots into one PDF. Word to PDFConvert a .docx into a clean, shareable PDF. Excel to PDFTurn spreadsheets into tidy tables that fit the page. PowerPoint to PDFTurn simple .pptx decks into a PDF, one page per slide.

Convert from PDF

PDF to JPGSave every page, or just some, as JPG or PNG images. PDF to WordGet the text out of a PDF into an editable .docx. PDF to ExcelPull tables and figures out of a PDF into a spreadsheet. PDF to textCopy all the text out of a PDF as plain text.

Private by design

Files are read and rebuilt on your device with open-source libraries like pdf-lib and PDF.js. Nothing is sent to a server, ours or anyone else’s.

Locked files

Password-protected or encrypted PDFs need unlocking first. Fillable form fields may not stay fillable after merging or splitting.

Big files

It runs on your machine, so very large PDFs (hundreds of pages, heavy scans) can take a moment. Your phone can do it too, just slower.
