Is this right for me?

It's built for coaches and consultants who sell offers worth thousands and have clients with a real before and after. It works best when those clients run real businesses in real places: a shop floor, a kitchen, a clinic, a studio. If a new client is worth a few hundred pounds to you, it isn't the right fit yet.

What if my clients don't want to be on camera?

Most people are nervous, not unwilling. The pre-interview is a relaxed phone call, and on the day it feels like a conversation, not a performance. Nobody's filmed without signing a release, and if a client says no, we choose another story.

How much of my time does it take?

A day and a half: the story call, your shoot day, and signing off the cuts. We book and run everything with your clients directly.

Where do you film?

Wherever the story is: your clients' shops, kitchens, clinics and sites. Travel within London and the South East is included. Further afield, we quote it before anything's booked.

Who owns the films?

You get a full licence, indefinitely, for every platform including paid ads. With your permission we'll also show them as our own work.

What if I don't like a film?

You sign off every film before it's final, and your clients approve their own story, so nothing goes live that anyone's unhappy with.

Will you make up results?

Never. We don't film income screenshots or invented numbers. Every story is true, told by the person it happened to, with their consent. It's also the version people believe.

What if the films don't get watched?

That's what the 30-day review is for. We look at what's being watched and what isn't, and adjust the placements, posts and emails within the launch. We won't promise you sales figures, because nobody honestly can.

Can I run the films as ads?

Yes. Your licence covers paid ads on every platform. If you'd like help running them, we can talk it through on the story call.

Is there a smaller version?

Yes. Pre-Sold Starter is your offer film and one client story film from a single shoot day, £3,450 + VAT. It suits you if you have one standout client story.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Three payments of £2,500, same total, no interest. Or half up front and half on delivery.
