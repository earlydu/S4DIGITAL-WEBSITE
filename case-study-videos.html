Case study video production, UK

Case study videos that show the result, in your customer’s words.

A case study video tells the story of one customer: what the problem was, what you did, and what changed. It’s filmed with them, where they work, so the proof comes from them and not from you. Our case study films start from £2,500 + VAT.

Plan a case study→ See examples→

More than a testimonial

A testimonial says they were great. A case study shows what the work did, with a before, an after and a result. It sits somewhere between a testimonial and a short documentary, and buyers trust it more because they can hold it up against their own situation.

How we make one

We start with a call to pick the right customer and agree what a good result looks like. Then we film an open interview on location, plus their business at work, so the story has something to look at. The edit is built around the result, and we cut shorter versions for LinkedIn, sales decks and your website.

For B2B and software companies

The buyer is asking one question: would this work for us? So the strongest case studies name the problem early, show the product in use and put the outcome on screen. A buyer comparing suppliers will watch a few minutes if it’s specific, and a 60 second version does the work in sales emails.

Questions people ask

More on this in our posts on customer testimonial videos and writing a video brief.

How much does a case study video cost?

Our case study films start from £2,500 + VAT. The price depends on the length, the number of shoot days and how many cut-downs you need, and we agree one fixed figure before anything is booked.

How long should a case study video be?

Most work best at two to four minutes for a website or a sales meeting, with a 60 second version for LinkedIn and sales emails. We usually cut both from the same shoot.

What’s the difference between a case study video and a testimonial?

A testimonial is an opinion: they were great. A case study is a story with a before, an after and a result. Buyers trust the second more because it gives them evidence they can hold up against their own situation.

Which customer should we film?

The one who got a clear result and is happy to talk about it. Enthusiasm matters more than polish. We help you choose on the first call, and we brief your customer so they know exactly what the day involves.

Do you script what the customer says?

No. We ask open questions and follow up on the answers, which is why people sound like themselves rather than like a brochure. Our free guide includes the question bank we use.

Do you only film in London?

We’re based in South London and film across the UK. Our case studies so far have taken us to Suffolk, Dorset, Hampshire and all over London.

Got a customer worth filming?

Tell us who they are and what changed for them. We come back within one working day.
