What a mixed shoot can look like

A typical mixed content shoot delivers 30 edited photographs and three short-form videos, built around the brief agreed before the day.

The mix can be flexed. A shoot built around video can trade photographs for an extra video, and a shoot built around photography can go the other way. We agree which way it leans before the day so you know exactly what you’re getting, and anything on top of that is quoted first.
