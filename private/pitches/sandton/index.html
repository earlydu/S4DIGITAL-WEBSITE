Outside Instagram

Your homepage opens on Tower Bridge. The first words are about the whole of the UK, and Morden isn't there.

The blog is good and local. Posts like Buying a Home in Morden in 2027 are the right topics. None of them has a video yet.

No YouTube or TikTok. That's where people go looking for area guides before they move.

Three names in use. Sandton Real Estate, Sandton Estate Agents and Sandton Chartered Surveyors.

Reviews are thin. We found very few on Google or the portals. A steady run of new ones from happy clients would set the tone quickly.

What's already working is you

You come across calm, clear and unhurried.

You answer questions the way you would across the desk, at a natural pace of about 140 words a minute. You don't oversell, and with people trusting you with the biggest thing they own, that matters.

So the content we'd make keeps your pace: short, steady answers from a chartered surveyor who knows Morden, filmed so they look and sound as good as the advice.

You're also posting every two or three days. Consistency is the hardest habit to build, and you already have it.

Six things we'd change

  1. 1

    Sound. Outdoor phone audio picks up wind and traffic. Clip-on mics and a quieter spot fix it.

  2. 2

    Framing. You're small in a wide shot, in the same spot each time. Closer shots and more places, the shop included.

  3. 3

    Show it. When you mention Cannon Hill Common or the regeneration, the viewer should see it.

  4. 4

    Lead with the answer. “One-bed flats in SM4 are going for about £245,000” stops a scroll faster than the question.

  5. 5

    Real Morden. Swap stock clips and AI images for your streets, your shop and your team.

  6. 6

    One voice. Captions in your own words and Sandton's purple, under one name everywhere.

How each month runs

  1. 1

    Planning call, week one. Thirty minutes to agree the month's topics and shoot dates.

  2. 2

    We do the prep. Scripts land on your phone five days ahead. On the day they run on our teleprompter, so nothing to print.

  3. 3

    Shoot. We arrive 30 minutes early and set up at the shop or on location. You're on camera for about two hours.

  4. 4

    Edits to approve. Within five working days, in a shared calendar you can check on your phone.

  5. 5

    Posted for you. A mix of videos, single graphics and carousels, scheduled across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube and Google.

  6. 6

    Monthly review. A one-page report on what worked and what we'll change next month.
