Camera kit hire in South London, from a working filmmaker.

This is the kit I shoot with: the Sony FX6 and A1, G Master lenses, Sennheiser sound, Amaran lights and the grip to hold it all. Collect from Crystal Palace, SE19, or I can deliver in London. Everything is booked through Scouty, which handles the payment and ID checks, and you’ll need hire insurance.

Browse the kit→ Hire me with it→

Dry hire

Take the kit and shoot it yourself. It goes out clean, charged and tested, and I’ll show you round it when you collect. The kits put together what usually gets hired together, so you’re not short a battery or a plate on the day.

Hire me with it

If you’d rather not shoot it yourself, book me as a self-shooting director with the kit. Interviews, documentary, events and brand content. Tell me about the job and I’ll come back with a price for the day.

Collection and delivery

Pick up and drop off in Crystal Palace, SE19. Evenings and weekends are fine, and for a weekend hire you can usually collect on Friday and drop back on Monday. I can deliver in London, just ask.

Questions people ask

How do I book?

Every item is listed on Scouty, so you book and pay there. Message me through the listing first so I can check the dates, then Scouty handles the payment and ID checks.

Do I need insurance?

Yes. I'll need your hire insurance certificate and photo ID before the first booking. Scouty doesn't insure the kit, so it has to be covered by your own hire policy.

Where do I collect from?

Crystal Palace, SE19. We'll agree a time that suits, and evenings and weekends are fine. I can deliver in London too, just ask.

Can I hire it for the weekend?

Usually, yes. For a weekend hire you can collect on Friday and drop back on Monday.

Is it cheaper for longer hires?

Yes, hires of five days or more get 20% off the day rate.

Can you come and shoot it as well?

Yes. I'm a working self-shooting director, so you can hire me with the kit for a crewed day. Tell me about the job and I'll come back with a price.

Need someone to shoot it?

Tell me about the job and the dates. I come back within one working day.
