Filming in West London: what to know.

  • Each borough runs its own filming.Kensington and Chelsea, Hammersmith and Fulham, Ealing, Hounslow and Richmond each handle filming separately. Film London points you to the right office, and we make the application.
  • Small crews have it easier.Film London’s guidance for a handheld crew of five or fewer that isn’t blocking the pavement is that formal permission usually isn’t needed, but the borough still wants at least three days’ notice.
  • The big parks are Royal Parks.Kensington Gardens, Hyde Park, Richmond Park and Bushy Park are Royal Parks, and commercial filming in them needs their own licence, separate from the council.
  • Indoors keeps it simple.Inside your shop, studio, kitchen or office, the council isn’t involved. Most interviews happen indoors, and we only take the camera onto the street when the story needs it.

Do I need a permit to film in West London?

On the street, usually notice rather than a full permit for a small handheld crew, but it depends on the borough. The Royal Parks need their own licence. Inside your premises, no council permit is needed. We handle it.

Have you filmed in West London?

Yes. We filmed a residential charging project at a development in Kensington for Easee, told by the residents and the managing agent.

Do you travel to West London?

Yes. We build travel into the one fixed figure, so there’s no surprise mileage on the invoice.

How much does a brand film cost in West London?

Our documentary brand films start from £15,000 + VAT, and most land between £15,000 and £25,000 + VAT. Every film is quoted as one fixed figure after a short call, because a one-location film and a multi-location film aren’t the same job. Research, shooting, edit, grade, mix and the full licence are all inside it. If a film isn’t the right spend yet, our one-off Spotlight shoot is £795 + VAT, a Content Day is £1,200 + VAT and case study films start from £2,500 + VAT.

How long does a brand documentary take?

It depends on the scope. A film can take days, weeks or months, from a single location shot in a few days to a story followed over a season. We set the timeline with you on the first call, and most of the quality lives in the part that can’t be rushed: the research before filming and the edit after it.

Can you film social media content for a West London business?

Yes. A Content Day is £1,200 + VAT and gives you a batch of short videos and photographs from one visit, edited and ready to post. If you’d rather not run the accounts yourself, our monthly plans cover the planning and posting too.
