Common questions

What kind of businesses do you work with?

+

Personal brands, coaches and consultants, founders, professional services, trades and construction, automotive businesses, fitness and combat sports, manufacturers and distributors, local businesses and established organisations. If it can be filmed or photographed, we can usually help.

Do I have to commit to a monthly retainer?

+

No. Plenty of clients start with a single Personal Brand Content Shoot or a one-off project and move into a monthly partnership later, once they know how much content they actually get through.

What does a content day actually produce?

+

It depends on the brief agreed beforehand. A mixed day usually produces a batch of edited photographs, several short-form videos and often one longer piece. We confirm the deliverables in writing before the shoot rather than promising the same numbers to everyone.

Do you handle the posting as well?

+

Yes, on a Content Partnership. That includes the content calendar, captions, scheduling and monthly reporting. On a one-off shoot you get the content ready to post and handle the posting yourself.

Is paid advertising included?

+

Not as standard. Advertising is an optional add-on for selected partnerships, managed by a specialist advertising manager, and advertising spend is paid separately by you.

Who actually turns up to the shoot?

+

Depending on the job, a trained camera operator or a small crew, working to a brief and creative direction set by Earl. Editing is handled by our editing team. Earl stays responsible for creative direction, strategy, quality control and delivery.

Do we get the raw files?

+

Not as standard. Raw photo and video files can be bought as an add-on. Everything usable from the agreed brief is delivered edited and ready to publish.

How far do you travel?

+

Travel is included within the agreed local South London area and quoted separately beyond that. We work across London and the UK regularly.
